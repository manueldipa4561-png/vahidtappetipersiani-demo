## Creative direction

Create a site that feels like a modern collector's gallery and specialist atelier rather than a generic furniture e-commerce template.

The visual language should combine:

- quiet luxury;
- Italian editorial restraint;
- tactile textile detail;
- warm museum/gallery lighting;
- heritage without visual nostalgia;
- contemporary grid and premium typography;
- deep oxblood / burgundy, warm cream, walnut, antique-gold accents, charcoal/ink;
- strong asymmetry and generous negative space;
- refined motion, never gimmicky.

Typography direction:

- expressive editorial serif for large titles;
- precise modern sans-serif for navigation, product data, forms and utility UI;
- strong size contrast, clean rhythm, excellent mobile legibility.

## Motion system

Use refined, performance-conscious motion:

- slow hero image drift / micro-zoom;
- reveal-on-scroll with subtle vertical movement;
- product image micro-scale on hover;
- underline/nav feedback;
- smooth drawer/modal transitions;
- respect responsive performance and accessibility.

Do not create excessive 3D or GPU-heavy motion unless it clearly improves the rug experience.
