Preferred concept:

- camera moves into macro scale;

- a damaged edge or fringe is visualized;
- the sequence shows a restrained transformation from worn / damaged state toward restored structure;
- weave geometry, thread tension and pile depth are emphasized;
- the final state returns to a full, beautifully presented textile;
- motion should feel educational, elegant and tactile rather than sci-fi.

### Preferred Higgsfield execution strategy

Use Higgsfield's **3D-capable workflow / 3D Jutsu tools** when available to produce an editable, lightweight 3D asset or animation suitable for website integration.

Priorities:

1. Inspect and plan before generating.
2. Use one coherent 3D scene instead of many disconnected clips.
3. Keep the scene lightweight enough for web use.
4. Export a GLB/compatible web asset when possible.
5. Integrate it with progressive enhancement and a non-WebGL fallback.
6. If a true interactive 3D asset cannot be delivered cleanly within tool or browser constraints, generate a loopable 3D-rendered cinematic sequence and integrate it as the fallback — but do not silently downgrade without documenting it.
7. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and mobile performance.
8. Never spend credits on duplicate attempts without first diagnosing the issue.

### 3D budget discipline

- Check cost before any expensive Higgsfield generation when cost-estimation is available.
- Use the smallest number of generations needed to achieve a premium result.
- Keep total cumulative Higgsfield use under **50 credits**.
- Preserve a safety buffer rather than targeting exactly 50.
- If the remaining budget is insufficient for a meaningful 3D result, stop before overspending and keep the best lightweight fallback.

General Higgsfield rules:

- no fake logos;
- no fake text baked into images;
- no plastic-looking fibers;
- no repeated identical rug patterns;
- no generic stock-photo aesthetic;
- preserve luxury commercial-photography standard;
- use realistic directional lighting and specific textile micro-texture;
- 2K output where budget permits;
- prefer fewer strong final assets over many variants.
